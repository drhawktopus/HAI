# Lab hardware provenance — where each machine and GPU came from (bought, business stock, cast-off)

**Source:** the owner, 2026-11-03 (correcting a public-page draft that got it wrong). The `Machine-*.md` docs record specs,
not acquisition history; this is the record for that. Nothing in the lab was bought for AI: it accumulated for other
needs over several years.

## Three origins

1. **Bought by the owner, for his own use** — the fleet-class machines: **Mothership** (gaming PC; the RX 7900 XTX was
   bought NEW), **BigHonker** (his desktop), **Softserve**. These are NOT cast-off hardware.
2. **Leftover stock from Catman's Classic Power Gaming LLC** (the PC-building business on his résumé, Oct 2022 – Apr
   2023; framing per `Career\GET-owner-RICH\Truth-Boundaries.md`). The business's cards came largely from his partner's
   former crypto-mining stock; when the business dissolved, the owner bought the remaining stock. **Most of the lab's NVIDIA
   cards come from here, including rigby's GTX 1060**, the RTX 2060 on the shelf, and the 3070-class cards. The
   hardware has been through several life cycles (mining → business stock → the lab).
3. **Cast-off gear** — yard sales, free bins, people the owner knows getting rid of old equipment: the lower-class hardware,
   **rigby and mordecai** (rigby arrived with no GPU — "Rigby came with nothing"), and probably the **GT 730** (it came
   with one of the cast-off machines, or someone just handed it over; the owner is not sure which).
